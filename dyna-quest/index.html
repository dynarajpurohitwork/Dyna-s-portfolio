What catches my attention?

Not big budgets or celebrities. It's risk — Flipkart's giant goalpost billboard, the campaigns where someone in the room said "this might fail" and shipped it anyway.

Zepto turning grocery drops into nostalgia. Blinkit building community through tiny surprises. Not selling — bonding.

How I break down a campaign

Business Goal → Audience → Human Insight → Platform → Creative → Distribution → Measurement → Iteration

Great content on the wrong platform is still the wrong content. Repeated too often, it becomes noise.

What I notice before everyone else

Photographic memory turned into pattern recognition — the tiny shift in behaviour, the effort behind something that looks effortless, why one post quietly built trust while another just went viral.

Marketing rarely wins alone. I'm already thinking design, ops, creators, timelines — every department aiming at one story.
